# Expedition & Character Cards

## Expedition card

- Type:
  There are 3 types of Expedition cards to help you on your journey:
  items,
  crew,
  and pets.
- Ability:
  Abilities can help you resolve events,
  purchase items,
  move around the board,
  etc.
- Cost:
  This is the price in coins you must pay to purchase the card.

## Character card

- Name:
  Your character's name.
- Abilities:
  Event bonuses
  and special actions that you may perform during the game.
