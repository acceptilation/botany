## Overview

Players assume the roles of Victorian-era plant hunters,
travelling the world in search of fame,
fortune,
and the coveted Queen's Prize in Botany.
As they travel the globe to collect specimens,
the players will need to manage their expedition funds,
experience thrilling events,
and acquire support for their journeys.
Whenever a player returns to their estate,
they will add new specimens to their collection and build new garden features,
such as orangeries and conservatories.
The player with the most points at the end of the game will earn the Queen's Prize in Botany and be declared the winner!
