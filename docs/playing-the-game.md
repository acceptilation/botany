# Playing the Game

## Goal

Players will compete to earn points by collecting specimens,
garden features,
and bonuses.
The player with the most points at the end of the game receives the Queen's Prize in Botany card
and wins!

## Gameplay

Players will take turns,
starting with the first player
and continuing clockwise,
until the end game conditions are met (see page 15).
On their turn,
a player will first pay for their expedition expenses,
then move around the map to collect specimens
or to return to their estate to display the specimens they have collected
and consolidate their gains.
The player will also collect income from their estate based on their reputation
before having an opportunity to invest their funds into improvements to their estate
or their expedition's equipment.
